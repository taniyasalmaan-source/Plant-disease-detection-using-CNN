About RoseGuard

Better plant care starts with curiosity.

AI-Based Rose Plant Disease Detection Using Deep Learning

RoseGuard is a rose leaf screening application that classifies images as healthy or unhealthy.

The project

A helpful guide for the everyday gardener.

Upload a rose leaf photo and a trained convolutional neural network classifies its appearance as healthy or unhealthy. An unhealthy result includes general symptoms, precautions, and guidance to seek a specific diagnosis from a local expert.

The binary model cannot name a disease or reliably reject unrelated photos. Model predictions are unavailable until a correctly labeled model has been trained. The current tiny dataset is for pipeline testing only and is insufficient for reliable training or validation.

✿

Project objectives

  • Make early visual screening approachable.
  • Offer clear, practical plant-care guidance.
  • Keep model confidence and limitations visible.
  • Support a repeatable, documented binary training workflow.
